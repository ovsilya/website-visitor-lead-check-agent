🎯 What counts as intent
The solution pages someone clicked, not a homepage drive-by. An AI-lab program manager reading robotics pages counts; a CEO who lands and leaves doesn't. RB2B has no time-on-page or scroll data, so which pages and how many are the measure.
🌍 Focus regions
US, Canada, UKI, Western Europe, SE Asia, Japan, Korea, China. Ireland is key: many tech clients have their HQ there. The region comes from city and state; outside the US, RB2B mostly sees companies, so those visits become account notes.
📅 Visit window
Repeat and recent visits are counted over a recent window: 2 weeks to start, adjustable to 10 days or 1 week once we see results.
✉️ Personal emails
Not a reason to drop a lead. The lead is kept and marked "find work email" (Apollo / ZoomInfo). Ruled out only when the personal email caused a wrong company match (e.g. live.com → Microsoft).
🏷️ RB2B scores and tags
Not used. RB2B's own scoring and "Hot lead" tags aren't trusted (64% of identified people carry "Hot lead"). to confirm with the analyst.
➕ Extra fields
Fields marked + weren't in Marketing's picks but are needed: visit dates for recent and repeat visits, city and state for regions.
Built on the legacy report: its target-account list and company matching (careers and subsidiary domains), his title rules as a starting point, and his principle that a week with zero leads is a valid result.

· Only yeses get points. Points rank leads; they never turn a no into a yes.
· Role counts once (2 or 1). Page types add up. Maximum 10 points.
· Each lead's "why" lists its points, e.g. Contact Us +3 · service page +2 · leads AI/data +2 = 7. Same file in, same order out.
· After the pilot: set a score threshold from real results, e.g. "above 67/100, client success acts on ~80% of leads".

  • Read the file and remove duplicates

4What you get: a short answer in the chat, and a weekly email

1
Upload the raw RB2B file in the chat, any time: weekly, daily, or any date range. Later: connect to RB2B directly instead.
2
Get a short answer by account tier: each tier lists its accounts, and each account its top leads, with clickable page links, a why and a next step.
3
Ask follow-ups or download the full list.
4
Weekly email of the report to one inbox, the lead triage team, who route each lead to the right client-success owner.
One or two good leads a week for an account like Initech is enough. A quiet week can simply say "nothing worth acting on this week".
